Lesson 7

Lesson 7: Dynamics and expression

Lesson 7 of 30, in Week 3 of Sound Detectives.

Learning intention

I can identify dynamics and expression and explain their effect.

Success criteria

tick them off as you go, they save on your device

Key word

Dynamics & expressive techniques

How loud or soft the music is, and the little performance tricks (sliding, plucking, shaking a note) that add feeling.

Listen

A film music cue with clear dynamic changes (quiet build to a loud climax).

What we are doing

  1. Recap: dynamics = volume; expression = the detail that gives a style its feel (articulation, accents).
  2. Listen to the film cue and follow a listening map of the dynamics.
  3. Mark where the music gets louder or softer and what happens to the mood.
  4. Add expression words (legato, staccato, accent) to your glossary.

Your task

On a listening map, track the dynamics of the cue and write one sentence on how a change in dynamics changed the mood.

Before you leave

Name one moment where dynamics created tension.
