Lesson 21

Lesson 21: Analysing a film cue

Lesson 21 of 30, in Week 7 of Sound Detectives.

Learning intention

I can analyse film music using the elements of music.

Success criteria

tick them off as you go, they save on your device

Listen

Hans Zimmer, 'Cornfield Chase' (Interstellar) — building dynamics and layered texture, rich material for a two-element analysis.

What we are doing

  1. Listen to the cue twice.
  2. Plan using element prompts.
  3. Write about how the composer manipulates two elements to support the scene.
  4. Check your evidence and terminology.

Your task

Write a response on how this film cue uses the elements of music to support the story.

Before you leave

Name the two elements you wrote about.
