Lesson 12

Lesson 12: Exam strategies and review

Lesson 12 of 30, in Week 4 of Sound Detectives.

Learning intention

I can use effective strategies in a listening exam.

Success criteria

tick them off as you go, they save on your device

What we are doing

  1. Learn the strategies: annotate while listening, use the replays, manage your time, use your terminology bank.
  2. Quick-fire revision of the six elements.
  3. Practise annotating a short excerpt in real time.
  4. Ask any final questions before the exam.

Listen

Grieg, 'In the Hall of the Mountain King' (Sydney Symphony Orchestra / Ashkenazy) — unseen, and short enough to annotate start to finish. Tempo, dynamics and texture all build together, so it gives real-time practice catching several elements changing at once.

Your task

Annotate a short excerpt as it plays, then plan (not write) a full response in two minutes.

Before you leave

Write down your personal exam plan in one sentence.

Slides and notes

  • Hand out the strategy checklist and terminology bank. Do a final quick-fire revision of all six elements.
