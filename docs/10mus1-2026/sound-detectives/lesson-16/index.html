Lesson 16: Jazz listening

Lesson 16 of 30, in Week 6 of Sound Detectives.

Learning intention

I can identify the features and context of jazz.

Success criteria

tick them off as you go, they save on your device

Listen

Vocal/standard: Ella Fitzgerald & Louis Armstrong, 'Summertime' — the classic jazz-standard vocal duo sound.

Listen

Big band: Glenn Miller, 'In the Mood' — the archetypal swing-era big band riff and ensemble sound.

Listen

Small-combo bebop: Charlie Parker, 'Ornithology' (1946) — fast bebop with an audible improvised alto solo.

What we are doing

  1. Learn the context: where and when jazz developed.
  2. Meet the key features: swing, improvisation, blue notes, instrumentation.
  3. Listen to the three excerpts and spot these features.
  4. Identify the instruments and where the improvised solos are.

Your task

For each excerpt, tick the jazz features you hear and mark where a solo is improvised.

Before you leave

How can you tell a section is improvised rather than written?
