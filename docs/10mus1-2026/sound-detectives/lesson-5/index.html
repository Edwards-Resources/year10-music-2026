What we are doing

  1. Recap: performing media = the sound sources; timbre = their tone colour.
  2. Listen and build a class ‘timbre word bank’ (bright, warm, harsh, mellow, etc.).
  3. Fill in the performing media and instrument family tables on this page as we go.
  4. Play an instrument ID quiz with short clips.
  5. Match a timbre word to each instrument you hear.

Your task

For each clip, name the instruments or voices and give one word for their tone colour.

Before you leave

Pick one instrument and describe its timbre in a full sentence.
