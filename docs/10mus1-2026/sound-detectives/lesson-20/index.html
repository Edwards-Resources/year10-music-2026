Lesson 20

Lesson 20: Film music practical / compose

Lesson 20 of 30, in Week 7 of Sound Detectives.

Learning intention

I can use an element of music to create a mood for a scene.

Success criteria

tick them off as you go, they save on your device

Listen

'Sintel' (Blender Foundation, Creative Commons) — mute the sound and cue a 20-30 second scene for students to score.

What we are doing

  1. Choose a clip or mood to score.
  2. Pick one element to focus on (e.g. tempo, timbre or dynamics).
  3. Create a short cue using flat.io, a DAW or instruments.
  4. Share and explain the effect you aimed for.

Your task

Create a 20-30 second cue that uses one element to set a clear mood for the scene.

Before you leave

Which element did you use, and what mood did it create?
