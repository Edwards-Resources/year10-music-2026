Lesson 28

Lesson 28: Synthesis presentation

Lesson 28 of 30, in Week 10 of Sound Detectives.

Learning intention

I can present a clear analysis of a work.

Success criteria

tick them off as you go, they save on your device

What we are doing

  1. In a small group, choose a work to analyse.
  2. Plan a short multimodal presentation (audio + key points).
  3. Present to the class.
  4. Give one strength and one question to each group.

Your task

Present your group analysis of how several elements create interest in your chosen work.

Before you leave

One thing another group did well in their analysis.

Slides and notes

  • Frame this as a model for how a listening Depth Study can be presented (including viva voce).
