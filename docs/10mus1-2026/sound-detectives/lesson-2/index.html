Lesson 2

Lesson 2: Duration and pitch (listen and play)

Lesson 2 of 30, in Week 1 of Sound Detectives.

Learning intention

I can identify duration and pitch features by ear.

Success criteria

tick them off as you go, they save on your device

Key word

Duration

How music moves through time: is it fast or slow, are the notes short or long, does it have a steady beat or does it feel off-kilter?

Key word

Pitch

How high or low the notes are, and what the melody does: does it climb, fall, stay put?

Listen

A short rhythmic example (clear beat and metre) and a short melodic example (clear melody) , one of each.

What we are doing

  1. Recap: duration = beat, tempo, metre, rhythm. Pitch = melody, contour, range.
  2. Write the definitions of duration and pitch in your books.
  3. Clap or play the rhythm patterns as a class.
  4. Play or sing the short melodies and describe their shape.
  5. Listen and identify these features by ear.

Your task

Notate or describe a 4-bar rhythm you hear, then describe the contour of a short melody (rising, falling, wave).

Before you leave

Was the melody’s range wide or narrow? How do you know?
