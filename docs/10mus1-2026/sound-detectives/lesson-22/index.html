Lesson 22: Music of Australia and First Nations music

Lesson 22 of 30, in Week 8 of Sound Detectives.

Learning intention

I can describe Australian music and its context respectfully.

Success criteria

tick them off as you go, they save on your device

Listen

Gurrumul, 'Wiyathul' — Yolŋu language, official video, gentle traditional-influenced example.

Listen

Baker Boy ft. Yirrmal, 'Marryuna' — contemporary First Nations hip-hop, official video, blends language and beats.

Listen

King Stingray, 'Get Me Out' — contemporary Yolŋu-language surf-rock, official video, broadens the styles on offer.

What we are doing

  1. Discuss what we mean by music of Australia, including First Nations music.
  2. Listen with respect and attention to context (cultural, historical, social).
  3. Note the instruments, language and stories where shared by the artists.
  4. Connect features of the music to its context.

Your task

For one work, describe two musical features and explain how they connect to the music’s context.

Before you leave

One thing you learned about the context of an Australian work.

Slides and notes

  • Use the ‘Music of Australia & First Nations Music’ deck (includes protocols).
  • Follow respectful protocols and ICIP awareness. Where possible, seek local community consultation and use artist-approved recordings. Avoid sacred or restricted material. Centre First Nations voices and artists.
