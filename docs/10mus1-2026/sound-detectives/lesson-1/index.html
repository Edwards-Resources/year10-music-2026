Lesson 1

Lesson 1: Active listening and the elements of music

Lesson 1 of 30, in Week 1 of Sound Detectives.

Learning intention

I can name the elements of music and use them to describe what I hear.

Success criteria

tick them off as you go, they save on your device

Listen

An engaging contemporary track to listen to first (teacher choice). Students just listen and jot what they notice.

What we are doing

  1. Listen to the track and jot down anything you hear.
  2. Meet the six elements of music: duration, pitch, texture, performing media & timbre, dynamics & expression, structure.
  3. Add each term to your listening glossary.
  4. Listen again and tag what you hear to an element.

Your task

Make a list of every element you can hear in the track, with one word describing how it is used.

Before you leave

Name three elements you heard today.

Slides and notes

  • Use the ‘Elements of Music’ deck to introduce the six elements.
