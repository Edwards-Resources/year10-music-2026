Sound Detectives

Aural skills and the elements of music · Term 3, 2026

Ten weeks of listening. You build a toolkit for describing what you hear, use it on jazz, film music and Australian music, and learn to write about music with evidence.

We are up to Lesson 6 of 30 Comparing 'Valerie' Lesson 6 runs Wednesday 5 August, Period 5. 5 lessons done so far.

Week 1

The Elements Toolkit

Welcome to our listening unit. This week we meet the six elements of music and learn to describe what we hear in clear, musical language.

  • 1Active listening and the elements of music
    I can name the elements of music and use them to describe what I hear.    done ✓
  • 2Duration and pitch (listen and play)
    I can identify duration and pitch features by ear.    done ✓
  • 3Writing about music 1
    I can write a clear sentence about how an element is used.    done ✓

Week 2

Texture, Timbre and Comparing

This week we listen across styles to explore texture and timbre, and learn how to compare two works using the elements of music.

  • 4Texture across styles (listen and build)
    I can identify and describe texture in different styles.    done ✓
  • 5Performing media and timbre
    I can identify performing media and describe timbre.    done ✓
  • 6Comparing 'Valerie'
    I can compare two versions of the same song using performing media and timbre.    ← we are here

Week 3

Dynamics, Structure and Exam Writing

This week we finish the toolkit with dynamics, expression and structure, and start writing full responses to the exam question.

  • 7Dynamics and expression
    I can identify dynamics and expression and explain their effect.
  • 8Structure
    I can identify the structure of a work by ear.
  • 9Writing about music 3: the exam question
    I can write an extended response on how elements create interest.

Week 4

Wide Listening and Exam Preparation

This week we listen widely across genres and rehearse the exam: full responses, feedback and strategies, ready for AT3 next week.

  • 10Wide listening carousel
    I can quickly identify style and elements across genres.
  • 11Extended response practice and feedback
    I can write to the standard of the AT3 task.
  • 12Exam strategies and review
    I can use effective strategies in a listening exam.

Week 5

AT3 Exam and Feedback

This week you sit the AT3 Listening Examination, then return to practical playing and review your responses to set goals.

  • 13AT3 Listening Examination
    I can analyse how the elements of music are manipulated and combined.
  • 14Practical recovery lesson
    I can contribute to a group performance with awareness.
  • 15Exam feedback and reflection
    I can evaluate my own listening response and set a goal.

Week 6

Jazz

This week we listen to jazz, play a 12-bar blues and improvise, then analyse how jazz uses the elements of music to create interest.

  • 16Jazz listening
    I can identify the features and context of jazz.
  • 17Jazz practical: 12-bar blues
    I can perform and improvise within a 12-bar blues.
  • 18Analysing a jazz recording
    I can analyse a jazz work using the elements of music.

Week 7

Film Music

This week we explore how film music creates mood and tells stories, create a short cue, and analyse a film composer’s choices.

  • 19Film music listening
    I can identify how film music supports narrative.
  • 20Film music practical / compose
    I can use an element of music to create a mood for a scene.
  • 21Analysing a film cue
    I can analyse film music using the elements of music.

Week 8

Music of Australia and First Nations Music

This week we listen to and analyse music of Australia, including music of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Peoples, with respect for context and protocols.

  • 22Music of Australia and First Nations music
    I can describe Australian music and its context respectfully.
  • 23Australian music analysis
    I can analyse an Australian work using elements and context.
  • 24Practical and response: Australian styles
    I can perform a feature of an Australian style and describe it.

Week 9

Comparative and Critical Listening

This week we compare how styles use the elements differently, evaluate interpretive choices, and start thinking about the Term 4 Depth Study.

  • 25Comparative listening across styles
    I can compare how an element is used across styles.
  • 26Critical listening: evaluating choices
    I can evaluate interpretive choices in performance.
  • 27Student-choice listening and Depth Study spark
    I can analyse music I choose and identify a personal area of interest.

Week 10

Synthesis and Depth Study Launch

This week we present our analyses, launch the Term 4 Depth Study, and finish with an approved proposal ready to develop.

  • 28Synthesis presentation
    I can present a clear analysis of a work.
  • 29Depth Study launch and idea generation
    I can generate realistic Depth Study ideas that match my interests and skills.
  • 30Depth Study proposal (milestone) and term celebration
    I can write a clear, realistic plan for my Depth Study.
