Lesson 29

Lesson 29: Depth Study launch and idea generation

Lesson 29 of 30, in Week 10 of Sound Detectives.

Learning intention

I can generate realistic Depth Study ideas that match my interests and skills.

Success criteria

tick them off as you go, they save on your device

What we are doing

  1. Learn what a Depth Study is and how it works.
  2. Explore the idea bank: performing, composing, listening and combinations.
  3. Brainstorm ideas that match your interests and skills.
  4. Shortlist two possible projects.

Your task

Write down two possible Depth Study projects and the focus area of each.

Before you leave

Which of your two ideas excites you most, and why?

Slides and notes

  • Optional: show the Depth Study idea bank from the Term 4 unit on the board.
  • Walk through the Depth Study: purpose, the 8-10 hours, and that students choose their focus. Explore a modelled work sample.
