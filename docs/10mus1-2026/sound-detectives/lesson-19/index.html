Lesson 19: Film music listening

Lesson 19 of 30, in Week 7 of Sound Detectives.

Learning intention

I can identify how film music supports narrative.

Success criteria

tick them off as you go, they save on your device

Listen

John Williams, 'Star Wars' Main Title — heroic, adventurous mood.

Listen

John Williams, 'Jaws' Theme — suspense and dread from a simple two-note idea.

Listen

Michael Giacchino, 'Married Life' (from 'Up') — poignant, emotional storytelling without dialogue.

What we are doing

  1. Meet the techniques: leitmotif, underscore, mickey-mousing.
  2. Listen to the cues and match musical features to on-screen meaning.
  3. Connect back to Term 1 program music and your Term 2 soundtrack task.
  4. Discuss how the elements signal emotion.

Your task

For each cue, link one musical feature to the emotion or action it supports.
