Lesson 9

Lesson 9: Writing about music 3: the exam question

Lesson 9 of 30, in Week 3 of Sound Detectives.

Learning intention

I can write an extended response on how elements create interest.

Success criteria

tick them off as you go, they save on your device

What we are doing

  1. Meet the exam-style question.
  2. Watch the teacher model a full response across several elements.
  3. Plan your response using a paragraph planner.
  4. Write a full timed response and self-assess against the criteria.

Your task

Write a full response to a short excerpt answering: how are the elements of music used to create interest?

Before you leave

Which element gave you the strongest point to write about?

Slides and notes

  • Introduce the AT3 exam question: ‘How are the elements of music used to create interest?’ Model a full response, thinking aloud.
