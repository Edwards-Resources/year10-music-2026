Lesson 25

Lesson 25: Comparative listening across styles

Lesson 25 of 30, in Week 9 of Sound Detectives.

Learning intention

I can compare how an element is used across styles.

Success criteria

tick them off as you go, they save on your device

Listen

Orchestra: Holst, 'Jupiter' (The Planets) — full, densely layered orchestral texture.

Listen

EDM: Avicii, 'Levels' — texture built and dropped through electronic layering.

Listen

A cappella: Pentatonix, 'Hallelujah' — texture created entirely from layered voices.

What we are doing

  1. Choose one element to focus on (e.g. texture).
  2. Listen to the three excerpts.
  3. Build a class comparison, then write your own.
  4. Use comparison language and evidence.

Your task

Compare how your chosen element is used across the three excerpts, with one similarity and two differences.

Before you leave

Which style used the element most surprisingly?
