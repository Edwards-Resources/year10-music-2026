Lesson 10

Lesson 10: Exam preparation: wide listening, strategies and a timed response

Lesson 10 of 28, in Week 4 of Sound Detectives.

Learning intention

I can use exam strategies and write to the standard of the AT3 task.

Success criteria

tick them off as you go, they save on your device

What we are doing

  1. Quick-fire listening: three short excerpts, style plus two elements for each.
  2. Learn the strategies: annotate while listening, use the replays, manage your time, use your terminology bank.
  3. Annotate the final excerpt in real time as it plays, three times, exactly as the exam runs.
  4. Plan, then write a full timed response to it.
  5. Read the feedback and the common misconceptions before Wednesday.

Listen

Quick-fire, world: Gamelan gong kebyar, 'Kapi Raja'. A distinctive percussive, metallic ensemble.

Listen

Quick-fire, classical: Vivaldi, Spring (The Four Seasons). Energetic Baroque strings.

Listen

Quick-fire, jazz: Take Five, the Dave Brubeck Quartet. An odd 5/4 metre.

Listen

Timed response: Grieg, 'In the Hall of the Mountain King' (Sydney Symphony Orchestra / Ashkenazy). Unseen, and short enough to annotate start to finish. Tempo, dynamics and texture all build together, so it gives real practice at catching several elements changing at once.

Your task

Annotate the final excerpt as it plays, then write a full timed response to it: three elements, one paragraph each, under exam conditions.

Before you leave

Write down your personal exam plan in one sentence.

Slides and notes

  • Bring your listening glossary and terminology bank. You get the strategy checklist at the start of the lesson.
  • The timed response runs under exam conditions, with the excerpt played three times and reading and writing time between playings, so Wednesday holds no surprises.
