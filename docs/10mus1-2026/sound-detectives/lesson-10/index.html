Lesson 10

Lesson 10: Wide listening carousel

Lesson 10 of 30, in Week 4 of Sound Detectives.

Learning intention

I can quickly identify style and elements across genres.

Success criteria

tick them off as you go, they save on your device

Listen

World: Gamelan gong kebyar, 'Kapi Raja' — a distinctive percussive, metallic ensemble.

Listen

Classical: Vivaldi, Spring (The Four Seasons) — energetic Baroque strings.

Listen

Film: Hedwig's Theme — John Williams, instantly recognisable orchestral timbre.

Listen

Jazz: Take Five — the Dave Brubeck Quartet, an odd 5/4 metre.

Listen

Contemporary: As It Was — Harry Styles.

What we are doing

  1. Rotate through the five listening stations.
  2. At each station, identify the style and two key elements.
  3. Log your answers as you go.
  4. Compare answers as a class at the end.

Your task

Complete your listening log: style + two elements + one effect for each of the five excerpts.

Before you leave

Which style was easiest to identify, and what gave it away?
