Depth Study

Your music, your project · Term 4, 2026

The last unit of Year 10, and the one you choose. You pick an area of music you care about, plan a project in performing, composing or listening, build it across Term 4, and present it to the class.

Before Term 4

Choosing and proposing

The work that decides how the whole unit goes. You choose a project that you actually care about and can actually finish, then write the plan that gets it approved.

  • 1Choose your project
    I can choose a depth study project I care about and can finish in the time I have.
  • 2Write your proposal
    I can write a proposal that sets out my goal, my product, my plan and how I will know it worked.

Term 4, Weeks 1 to 3

Developing it

The longest stretch of the unit. You build your product, get feedback twice, and keep a running record of your thinking so the write-up for your presentation is easy at the end.

  • 3Develop it
    I can work steadily on my project, use feedback to improve it, and keep a process journal that shows my thinking.

Term 4, Weeks 3 to 4

Refining and finalising

You compare your work honestly against the rubric for your focus area, fix the highest-impact gaps first, and check your submission is actually ready before the due date.

  • 4Refine and finalise
    I can check my project against the rubric for my focus area, fix the gaps that matter most, and get it ready to submit.

Term 4, Weeks 4 to 5

Presenting it

You submit your product, present it to the class with a short spoken introduction, and then take your turn as an audience member for everyone else's presentations.

  • 5Present it
    I can introduce my project to the class, present it clearly, and give useful feedback as an audience member.

AT4 Depth Study

Term 4, Week 4 (2 to 6 November 2026)

Your final product, presented in class. 20 marks, worth 25%. You are marked on what you make and how you present it, using the rubric for the focus area you chose. There is no written report.

The task notification is in Canvas. Your outcomes depend on the focus area you choose.

Outcomes

  • MU5-PER-01 performs repertoire with stylistic awareness and musical expression
  • MU5-PER-02 manipulates and combines the elements of music in performance to communicate musical ideas
  • MU5-COM-01 improvises, arranges or composes with stylistic understanding and musical expression
  • MU5-COM-02 manipulates and combines the elements of music to create musical ideas
  • MU5-LIS-01 uses listening skills to analyse music in relation to stylistic, cultural, historical and social contexts
  • MU5-LIS-02 uses listening skills to evaluate how the elements of music are manipulated and combined

Class material. Assessment tasks, submissions and marks stay in Canvas.
