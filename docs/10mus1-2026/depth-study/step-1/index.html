Step 1

Step 1: Choose your project

Step 1 of 5, worked on in Term 3, Weeks 9 and 10.

Learning intention

I can choose a depth study project I care about and can finish in the time I have.

Success criteria

tick them off as you go, they save on your device

Key word

Depth study

A self-directed music investigation in an area of personal interest. You choose the area, you choose the product, and you drive the work. This is the most independent unit of Year 10.

What you could do

Three focus areas, and a fourth option that combines two of them. Each row is the same project at three sizes, so find the one that matches where you actually are. You can also propose your own idea, as long as it fits a focus area.

  • Performing

    • EntryPrepare and perform one piece on your instrument, with short program notes.
    • CorePerform a set of two or three linked pieces, or record a multitrack cover of a song you love.
    • ExtensionLead a small ensemble, or perform a demanding programme you have chosen and rehearsed yourself.

    You hand in a live or recorded performance, plus program notes or a rehearsal log.

  • Composing

    • EntryWrite a short original piece or song section and explain the choices you made.
    • CoreWrite an original song and record a demo, or arrange an existing song in a new style.
    • ExtensionCompose to a brief: music for a film scene, a game, an artwork, or a produced track.

    You hand in a recording or demo, plus a score or lead sheet if your piece has one, and a short rationale.

  • Listening and Investigation

    • EntryA short presentation analysing how the elements are used in a track you already love.
    • CoreA multimodal investigation of an artist, a genre or a soundtrack, with examples.
    • ExtensionA viva voce where you argue a case about a style and answer questions on it.

    You hand in a multimodal presentation or a viva voce, with musical examples you play.

  • Combination

    • EntryCompose a short piece and then perform it yourself.
    • CoreInvestigate a style, then perform or compose something in it.
    • ExtensionResearch, compose and perform, and explain how each part fed the next.

    You hand in a product from each area you choose, plus a short piece of writing linking them.

How to choose well

  1. Interest. Pick something you actually care about. You are going to live with this for a term, and it shows in the final product when you do not.
  2. Skill. Pick something you can already do most of, and stretch the last part. A project that needs a skill you have not started learning is a project you will not finish.
  3. Time. You have eight to ten hours of class time and the weeks between. Halve your first idea if it does not fit that, and do the smaller thing properly.

Your task

Write down two projects you would genuinely like to do, one from each of two different focus areas. For each one, note what you would hand in and the single hardest part. Bring both to your proposal conference: choosing between two real options is much easier than inventing one on the spot.

Before you leave

In one sentence: what do you want to make, and why that one?
